================================================================================
MEMORY SAVINGS ACHIEVED
================================================================================

Baseline (No ZeRO):
  Parameters:      4 bytes/param
  Gradients:       4 bytes/param
  Optimizer (Adam): 8 bytes/param (2 states)
  Total:          16 bytes/param

ZeRO Stage 1:
  Parameters:      4 bytes/param
  Gradients:       4 bytes/param
  Optimizer (Adam): 2 bytes/param (partitioned)
  Total:          10 bytes/param
  Reduction:      ~4x for optimizer states

ZeRO Stage 2:
  Parameters:      4 bytes/param
  Gradients:       1 bytes/param (partitioned)
  Optimizer (Adam): 2 bytes/param (partitioned)
  Total:           7 bytes/param
  Reduction:      ~8x total

ZeRO Stage 3:
  Parameters:      1 bytes/param (partitioned)
  Gradients:       1 bytes/param (partitioned)
  Optimizer (Adam): 2 bytes/param (partitioned)
  Total:           4 bytes/param
  Reduction:      Nx (where N = world size)

With FP16 + ZeRO Stage 3:
  Total:           2 bytes/param
  Reduction:      Up to 16x vs baseline
